{{APP_NAME}} is built to work without collecting anything about you. This policy covers the {{APP_NAME}} app for iPhone and Android and the web version at {{SITE_URL}}.

What we collect

Nothing. {{APP_NAME}} has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and we don't share or sell any data. The app works offline and doesn't send anything over the internet.

What stays on your device

Your choices (mode, breathing pace, session length, sound and haptics) and whether you've seen the welcome screen are saved in the app's storage on your device, so they're still there next time.

{{APP_NAME}} also keeps a history of your sessions on your device: the date, length, mode and pace, and, if you use check-ins, the 0 to 10 ratings you give before and after. It's there so you can see what works for you in Activity. It never leaves your device, and we can't see it.

You can clear your session history at any time in Activity. Deleting the app, or clearing this site's data in your browser, removes everything.

Motion sensors

To draw the horizon and show motion readings, {{APP_NAME}} reads your device's motion and orientation sensors while it's open. Those readings are used on your device in real time and are never stored or sent anywhere.

The web version

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We don't receive or use that information.

Children

{{APP_NAME}} doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.
