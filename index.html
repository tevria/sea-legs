For true ship readings, lay the phone flat with the top toward the bow, or brace it upright facing forward. Handheld, your own movement mixes in. A longer roll period than usual means the ship is sitting more tender.

Quick fixes on deck

  • Eyes on the horizonGet outside and fix on the horizon or shoreline. Beats any screen.
  • Midships and lowLeast motion near the ship's centre of rotation.
  • Fresh airFace into it. Stay clear of exhaust and galley smells.
  • Small, dry foodCrackers or toast. An empty stomach makes it worse.
  • GingerChews, capsules or real ginger ale. Modest but real evidence.
  • P6 pressure pointThree finger widths below the wrist crease, between the tendons. Press firmly a few minutes each side.
  • Sip waterSmall and often. No big gulps.
  • Lie down, eyes closedIf it gets bad, cutting out the visual mismatch can reset you.

Gravol (dimenhydrinate) or a scopolamine patch work best taken before you sail and can make you drowsy. Check your company's fitness-for-duty rules before taking anything on watch.
